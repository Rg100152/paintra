Our story

Not a print shop.
A quiet archive.

PAINTRA began with a simple feeling: the art we kept returning to deserved to live somewhere other than a screen. So we gathered five distinct voices — cinematic, ethereal, mythic, atmospheric, expressive — and gave each body of work a proper home.

Every piece is printed to order, in small numbers, on paper chosen for its warmth and weight. Nothing is mass-produced. Nothing is disposable. The result is a collection you can build slowly, and live with for years.
